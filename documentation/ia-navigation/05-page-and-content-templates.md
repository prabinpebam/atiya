# Page and content templates

These templates specify information order, not visual layout.

## Home

1. **Positioning**
   - Current role or professional identity
   - Domain and scope
   - Distinctive value in one or two sentences
2. **Featured evidence**
   - Two or three outcomes or case studies
   - Mix of product, systems, and leadership evidence
3. **Practice**
   - Four practice areas
   - One-line promise for each
4. **Leadership**
   - Leadership proposition
   - Link to principles and one evidence item
5. **Current context**
   - Brief biography or current focus
6. **Contact**
   - Clear reason and way to connect

## Work index

1. Title and editorial premise
2. Featured case-study collection
3. Optional filter controls
4. Additional work or archive
5. Confidentiality note, if it helps explain the level of detail

Each card:

- Case-study title
- One-sentence outcome or tension
- Primary practice area
- Role phrase
- Representative media

## Case study

### Scan layer

The first screenful or content block should include:

- Title
- One-sentence premise
- Outcome summary
- Role and responsibility
- Team or collaboration context
- Time frame
- Primary and secondary practice areas

### Deep-story layer

Use this narrative order unless the story requires a justified variation:

1. Context and stakes
2. Problem or opportunity
3. Personal mandate and boundaries
4. Constraints and tensions
5. Key decisions
6. How the work unfolded
7. Collaboration and leadership
8. Result and evidence
9. Reflection: lessons, trade-offs, or what changed next

Avoid turning the story into a generic process checklist. Emphasize the decisions that changed direction or outcome.

### End matter

- Contributions and methods
- Tools, only when relevant
- Related practice-area links
- Two or three related case studies
- Clear route back to Work

## Expertise overview

1. Explanation of the broad practice
2. Four practice areas
3. How the areas reinforce each other
4. Representative cross-disciplinary case study
5. Route to Work

## Practice-area page

1. Practice-area promise
2. Point of view
3. Capabilities in plain language
4. Representative questions or situations
5. Selected evidence
6. Related methods
7. Connection to other practice areas

This page should not be a software proficiency list or a gallery detached from outcomes.

## Leadership overview

1. Leadership proposition
2. Scope and operating context
3. Operating principles
4. Hiring and team-building approach
5. Culture and capability development
6. Strategic influence and design-led initiatives
7. Evidence: leadership case studies, outcomes, or approved testimonials
8. Reflection and ongoing learning

## Leadership topic

1. Principle or claim
2. Why it matters
3. Observable behavior
4. Mechanisms, rituals, or tools
5. Example and result
6. Trade-offs or failure modes
7. Related evidence

## About

1. Short professional biography
2. Career arc told through themes, not a duplicate resume
3. Current role and areas of focus
4. Values or working principles
5. Selected personal context, if relevant
6. Resume, professional profiles, and contact
7. Views-are-my-own statement when appropriate

## Note

1. Title and concise summary
2. Publication and update dates
3. Main argument or observation
4. Supporting examples
5. Practical takeaway or open question
6. Related work and expertise

Notes should develop a point of view. They should not exist only to improve search visibility.
