# Content inventory and mapping

## Inventory worksheet

Create one row for every candidate story before selecting case studies.

| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Working title | Use an outcome or challenge, not an internal codename |
| Public context | Organization or product name that may be disclosed |
| One-line story | Situation, intervention, and result |
| Personal role | What was led, decided, designed, built, or enabled |
| Team context | Disciplines, partners, and leadership relationships |
| Primary practice area | Select one |
| Secondary practice areas | Select up to two |
| Contributions | Use the controlled vocabulary |
| Outcome types | Select one or two |
| Evidence available | Metrics, artifact, quote, public announcement, observation |
| Media available | Images, diagrams, video, prototype, code |
| Confidentiality | Public, redacted, summary only, private only, not publishable |
| Story strength | Strong, promising, weak |
| Missing material | What must be sourced, recreated, approved, or written |
| Intended audience | Primary audience for this story |
| Status | Candidate, selected, drafting, review, approved, published |

## Case-study selection matrix

Score candidates from 1 to 5.

| Criterion | Question | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Outcome clarity | Can a visitor understand what changed? | 3 |
| Leadership signal | Does it show judgment, influence, or team impact? | 3 |
| Personal contribution | Is the individual role specific and credible? | 3 |
| Story quality | Are there meaningful constraints, decisions, and trade-offs? | 2 |
| Public evidence | Can enough be shown or stated safely? | 2 |
| Craft evidence | Does it demonstrate quality in making? | 1 |
| Portfolio coverage | Does it add evidence not already covered elsewhere? | 2 |

Select three to five deep case studies. A balanced initial set should aim to cover:

- a product or end-to-end experience story;
- a systems, frontend, prototype, or AI-workflow story;
- a strategy or design-led initiative;
- a leadership, culture, hiring, or capability-building story;
- optionally, a motion, 3D, film, or spatial story with strong relevance.

One case can satisfy several of these needs.

## Evidence ladder

Prefer evidence higher on this ladder:

1. Publicly verifiable result or approved metric
2. Relative or normalized outcome
3. Public launch, adoption, or organizational change
4. Approved testimonial or collaborator observation
5. Specific behavior change or decision enabled
6. Artifact with an explanation of its effect
7. Process description without demonstrated effect

A polished artifact alone is not sufficient evidence for a senior-level case study.

## Confidential work treatment

For each selected story:

1. Determine what is already public.
2. Separate personal contribution from team and company claims.
3. Replace confidential values with approved relative language where permitted.
4. Redraw sensitive diagrams schematically rather than blurring screenshots.
5. Remove customer data, internal names, unreleased features, and identifying details.
6. Record approval status in the inventory.
7. If the remaining story is too vague to be credible, keep it for private discussion rather than publishing it.
