# Gallery: plan and Definition of Done

How the [spec](spec.md) is built. The first build, G1 to G10, made an album a page of kind `gallery` and is recorded in the [spec's As built](spec.md#20-as-built); its site side (the band, the album page, the lightbox, zoom, private albums, the planet) stays. The redesign, A1 to A6, makes an album its own resource with its own screen and editor, the way Google Photos and Apple Photos keep albums ([spec §21](spec.md#21-from-the-first-build)). Each phase leaves the site, edit mode and the planet working and deployable, and ends with its checks passing and a merge. The order follows what the owner can use soonest: albums as files the site reads, then the editor to make and fill them, then the viewer and its words, then the cover, the library and the rest.

Throughout, every choice is automatic first and his to override ([spec §18](spec.md#18-automatic-and-where-to-override-it)): each phase builds the automatic answer with the feature, and its override and Back to automatic with the screen that edits it.

> **TL;DR.**
> - **Status:** G1 to G10 built (22 October 2026). The album redesign specified (23 October 2026); A1 to A6 wait on the owner's sign-off of the spec.
> - **A1: the album resource.** `content/albums/<id>.json` and its contract (V38 to V55 restated, V52 to V55 new), the structure's album item, the words and the fingerprint on the picture's sidecar, linked videos as library items, `albumOrder` and `albumsOrder`, the new `AlbumLayout`, the album block by ID, the fixtures rewritten as album files, and the kind `gallery` retired.
> - **A2: the Albums screen and the album editor.** Every album as a grid of covers, Add album, and inside one its words typed in place, its items in a grid to select, sort and drag, text cards, uploads anywhere in the background, the picker, Settings and Publish.
> - **A3: the viewer and the words.** An item opened large with `MediaInfo` beside it, actions on a selection, pasting a YouTube or Vimeo link, duplicates found by fingerprint, and the alt text review.
> - **A4: the cover.** Set as cover, and Edit cover: the first build's arranger, in place on the album editor.
> - **A5: the library.** The Media screen on `PhotoGrid` and `MediaInfo`, with Used in, the albums a picture is in, and the same words.
> - **A6: private albums and the rest.** Private album files and their access, grants that name albums, the planet, telemetry and search for the resource, and the docs.

## 1. Phases

### A1: the album resource (U1, U5, U6, U8, U15)

1. **The contract** (`schema.ts`), as [spec §3](spec.md#3-the-model):
   - the `album` resource: `id`, `slug`, `title`, `summary`, `description`, `status`, `createdAt`, `updatedAt`, `publishedAt`, `topics`, `order`, `items`, `cover`, and the settings `layout`, `captions`, `dates`, `download` and `large`;
   - an item: `{id, media, download?, autoplay?, loop?}` or `{id, text}`;
   - the cover: `{lead}` or `{tiles, phone?}`, the tile as built;
   - the sidecar gains `description`, `place` and `fingerprint` (and a video's `taken`); a linked video is a sidecar of its own kind;
   - the structure's `item: {type: "album", id}`, and a section's `albumOrder`;
   - an article's `album` field and the kind `gallery` refused (V43).
2. **`src/site/content/album.ts`:** the rules V38 to V55, `albumOrder` (an album's order of items), `albumsOrder` (the gallery's order of albums), the cover's default with its lead, and the cover picture; the first build's presets, `phoneTiles`, reading order, counts and span of dates renamed from showcase to cover.
3. **The repository and the loader:** read `albums/` (and, through the private root, `private-pages/albums/`), resolve album nodes, and hand albums to every reader of items: cards, Related, the home views, search and the planet's frame. `loadContentLeavingOut` leaves out a broken album like a broken article.
4. **The site:** `AlbumLayout` draws the album page (the opening, the introduction, `AlbumGrid` with text cards, the end); `IndexLayout`'s `albums` view reads album files in `albumsOrder`; `ArticleLayout` loses its album branch; an album's card and social picture are its cover picture; the album block names an album ID (V51).
5. **The library:** linked videos made sidecars, with their posters as the site's own pictures; the fingerprint written for every upload, and for the library's existing items by a one-off script; a video file's date from its `creation_time`.
6. **The fixtures** rewritten as album files (`fixture-album`, `fixture-captions`, `fixture-story`, the private one), their items' words moved into their pictures' sidecars; their pictures stay where they are (media IDs never change).
7. **The first build's model removed:** the article's `album` field, an item's own words and `embed`, `showcase`. Edit mode's Items and Up front views keep working on the fixtures until A2 replaces them, then go.

### A2: the Albums screen and the album editor (U9, U11)

[Spec §9.1 to §9.4 and §9.7](spec.md#9-edit-mode):

1. **The Albums screen** (`/_edit/albums/`, `AlbumsScreen`): every album as a card (its cover picture, title, count, span of dates and tags), in the gallery's order with drafts first; find and filter; each card's menu (Open, Open on the site, Copy its address, Delete, which keeps the pictures); Sort for the gallery's order, and dragging a card for Your order; the side navigation's Albums entry, in `SCREENS`.
2. **Add album** makes an untitled draft at once in the section whose view is Albums and opens it with its title selected; files dropped on the screen make an album from them, titled from their dates.
3. **The album editor** (`/_edit/albums/<id>/`): `AlbumHeader` (the title and Add, Sort, Edit cover, Settings, Preview and Publish), `AlbumWords` (the title, short description and introduction typed in place, saved as typed, and the line of facts), the automatic cover drawn as its band, and `AlbumItems` on the kit's new `PhotoGrid`.
4. **Selecting and ordering** (`PhotoGrid`): a click, Shift and Ctrl choose; the keys move the focus and the selection; a drag or Alt with an arrow moves; dragging switches the album to its own order, and Sort (Oldest first, Newest first) puts it back; Remove from album keeps the file in the library.
5. **Adding:** files dropped anywhere on the editor upload in the background with their progress in the status line, each read at upload (date, focus, fingerprint, drawing or picture); Add from the library (the picker on `PhotoGrid`); a text card between items, which makes the order the owner's own (V53).
6. **Settings** (`AlbumSettings`, [§9.2.1](spec.md#921-settings)): the layout, captions, dates, downloads and large pictures, the address, the section, the access, the topics; Delete album.
7. **Saving:** through the store, with versions, the content check before writing, undo for every change, and `saveStatus`; `server/albums.ts` gives the screen's cards and the editor's thumbnails, counts and weight.

### A3: the viewer and the words (U7, U9)

[Spec §9.4, §9.5 and §9.8](spec.md#95-the-viewer-and-the-words):

1. **The viewer** (`AlbumViewer`): an item opened large, Previous and Next by keys, and the kit's new `MediaInfo` beside it: caption, description (rich text), alt text with Suggest, date taken and place, the item's download, a video's Play by itself and Loop, Make it standard, and the other albums and pages it's in. Every field is the picture's, saved to its sidecar.
2. **The selection's actions:** Add to cover, Set as cover, Add to album, Set the date, Allow or stop downloads, Remove from album.
3. **Linked videos:** Add → YouTube or Vimeo link, and a pasted address anywhere on the editor, made a library item through `albumEmbed.ts`; one already in the library is reused.
4. **Duplicates:** an upload whose fingerprint is in the library adds the picture already there, and says so (V55).
5. **Alt text:** published albums need it (V54); the facts line counts what's missing, and Review steps through those pictures in the viewer, with suggestions where the owner's AI service is on.

### A4: the cover (U10, U11, U12, U13)

[Spec §3.6 and §9.6](spec.md#96-the-cover):

1. **Set as cover** on an item, in the viewer or for a one-item selection: it sets `cover.lead`, and the automatic cover leads with it.
2. **Edit cover** (`AlbumCover`): the first build's arranger canvas, toolbar and keys in place on the album editor's band, its tray now the album's grid below; Use automatic cover and Done.
3. **The phone's view** and Arrange for phones, each tile's focus, Play in the band and Hide on phones, as built in G6.

### A5: the library (U9)

[Spec §9.9 and §9.10](spec.md#99-the-library):

1. **The Media screen** on `PhotoGrid` and `MediaInfo`: the same selection, the same words and facts, Used in with the albums a picture is in, and Add to album for a selection.
2. **The library's own views:** the timeline by month taken, the filters (Not in any album, In albums, Needs alt text, the kinds), Add link, Set the date and Set the place for a selection; deleting a picture says which albums it leaves first.
3. **Other screens** ([§9.10](spec.md#910-the-other-screens)): Sections lists albums among a section's pages (tagged Album) with the gallery's order in its Settings; the article editor's Album block chooses from the site's albums; the action center finds albums and Add album.

### A6: private albums, the planet and the rest (U15)

[Spec §12 to §14](spec.md#12-private-albums):

1. **Private albums:** `private-pages/albums/<id>.json`, made private or open in Settings (the file and its own media move folders in one transaction); a grant's scope names albums as it names pages; sealing, the leak check and the "protected content" tests on the album resource (V44, V52).
2. **The planet:** the Amphitheater's frame shows the album page from the album resource.
3. **Telemetry, search and sharing:** `lightbox_opened` as before; an album page's head for search engines and social sites: its canonical address, title, summary and cover picture.
4. **The docs:** the spec's As built, the related specs, AGENTS.md.

### The first build: G1 to G10

Built 22 October 2026: the model and the media (G1), the Albums view (G2), the album page (G3), the lightbox (G4), edit mode's Items (G5) and Up front (G6) views, private albums and the planet (G7), zoom (G8), the album block (G9) and suggested alt text (G10). What each phase made, and where it differed from its first spec, is in the [spec's As built](spec.md#20-as-built); what of it stays and what the redesign replaces is in [§21](spec.md#21-from-the-first-build).

## 4. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The lightbox's component is near its 500-line limit | G4 moves its script out first, with its rules pure, before adding anything |
| History in the planet's frame: a pushed state would make Back leave the building | No history in the frame; a test for Back on the planet with the lightbox open |
| The section page grows heavy with many albums | Tiles ask for their own sizes, later bands are lazy, nothing moves; measured against the budget in G2 |
| Decrypting a fetched private album page | The sealed runtime already decrypts sealed regions by key; if it can't take a fetched page, the lightbox falls back to the band's tiles, which the spec allows |
| A hostile SVG | Drawn only with `<img>` (no scripts run), and sanitized before it's saved; the test set grows with every case found |
| GitHub Pages' 1 GB limit | The build's weight check, warning at 750 MB; the album's weight shown in edit mode |
| APNG loses its frames | The upload form says so before saving, with what to do |
| The sealer misses a new attribute and leaks a media address | The leak check covers the new attributes, and a private fixture album is in the "protected content" tests |
| Square cells make very tall bands on very wide screens | The band takes the wide width, not the screen's, and edit mode warns past 8 rows |
| Two orders (the album's and the cover's) confuse | Edit cover says it changes only the cover; the counter and the filmstrip always follow the album's |
| Tiles that play make the section heavy or restless | Still unless the owner sets a tile to play; at most 2 in a band, 10 MB each, loaded only when near and played only in view; a warning past 4 MB; never under reduced motion or Save-Data; the band's Pause |
| A camera's clock was wrong, so dates and Sort by date taken are wrong | The date is shown in the details and can be changed or cleared; Sort is one change, undone with Undo; dates are shown to readers only when the album asks |
| Measuring the focus point moves crops already in use | Only new uploads are measured; a picture already in the library keeps its own until the owner asks Find the focus |
| Suggested alt text sends a picture away, or says who someone is | Only to the service the owner chose, at 960 px without its name; a private album's pictures only to a service that may get private pages; the rules ask for what's visible, never who; nothing is saved until he accepts or edits it |
| Zoom fights the swipe between items and the browser's own pinch | Swiping moves only when not zoomed; zoom is offered only where it's worth it, and the browser's pinch works elsewhere; tested with real touch events |
| A second item type breaks a reader of items that only knew articles (cards, Related, the home views, search, the planet's frame, the sealer, Save to remote) | A1 lists every reader and gives each a test with an album in it; `build-parity.mjs` holds the owner's build |
| Words on the picture change everywhere it's used, and that surprises | The viewer lists where else the picture is ("Also in Monsoon walks") beside the words; an article figure keeps its own caption |
| Hundreds of files dropped at once stall the editor | Uploads run in the background a few at a time, with their progress in the status line; the grid shows each as it lands; a failed one says why and can be tried again |
| The album editor outgrows the editor's part limits | Its parts are split from the start (header, words, items, viewer, cover, settings), and the shared grid and panel are kit pieces |
| Retiring the kind `gallery` breaks something the first build left | No real content uses it; the fixtures move in A1, and row 49 checks nothing of the old model is left |
| A phone arrangement drifts from the wide band | Kept in step on every tile added or removed wide, with a note; Back to automatic is always there |
| An album placed in another page is later made private, set back to draft or deleted | V51 runs over the whole site before every save, so the change is refused with the pages that show it; the block never points at nothing |
