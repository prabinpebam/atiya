# Home views for every site

The home views were designed on one site, the owner's: a design leader with six capabilities, a résumé and colleagues' recommendations. Atiya makes sites for anyone ([Atiya](../atiya.md)), so the same views have to work for a student with three projects, a freelancer winning clients, a writer with two hundred notes, and for him. This page turns his capability spine into a model any site can fill: a **goal**, **three to six strengths**, and **proof** found in what the person has already published. How the proof is found is [Curating the home page](curation.md); what the person does and sees is [Making your home page](owner-flows.md). It's part of [Home page views](README.md).

> **TL;DR.**
> - **One model for every site:** a goal (Get hired, Win clients or Share my work), 3 to 6 strengths, and proof that the rules find in the site's own pages. In edit mode, capabilities are called **strengths**, the word a person uses about themselves; the data keeps `capabilities`.
> - **The goal sets the defaults:** the two always-on actions, the question set, the lens presets, which kinds of proof count most and the default view. It never limits what a person can change.
> - **3 to 6 strengths:**
>   - with fewer than 3, the home stays the Classic hub;
>   - with more than 6, the first screen can't show every strength at a readable size on a phone, which breaks the coverage rule.
>   - Every view adapts its layout to 3, 4, 5 or 6 (§4).
> - **Every view has a minimum** (Spotlight needs 4 highlights with pictures, Proof grid 3 kinds of proof): below it, the view isn't offered, rather than shown half-empty (§5).
> - **The home doesn't grow with the site:** L1 and L2 have fixed budgets; only L3 grows, and only inside its drawer (§6).
> - **Setup asks nothing new** (promise P2): a new site starts on the Classic hub, and edit mode offers "Make your home page" once there's enough to show (§8).

## 1. Who makes a home page

Atiya's people ([desktop spec §2](../desktop/spec.md#2-who-and-the-jobs-they-hire-it-for), jobs J1, J3, J5 and J7), seen through the home page.

| # | Who | Their site | What their home must do | What's hard for them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | **A newcomer:** a student, a junior designer, someone changing careers | 1 to 5 pages, often the starter's samples at first | Show potential: a few projects and how they think | Little proof, no recommendations yet, and no idea what to call their strengths |
| O2 | **An experienced job hunter:** a senior designer, a manager, a leader | 10 to 60 pages, a résumé, recommendations | Be shortlisted for a role: range and depth at once | Much of the best work is confidential, and the range is wider than one screen (the owner's case) |
| O3 | **A freelancer or a small studio** | Case studies, clients, testimonials | Win a project: what I do, for whom, with what results, how to start | Proof is per client and per outcome; "Read résumé" is the wrong action |
| O4 | **A maker, writer or speaker** | 40 to 500 pages: notes, talks, side projects | Show the body of work and its themes; be read, followed, invited | Volume: the best work is buried under the newest |
| O5 | **The owner** | 17 articles, a résumé, recommendations | The worked example in every view page | AI transformation and product strategy rest mostly on résumé lines ([foundation §4](foundation.md)) |
| O6 | **An agent,** working for any of them | Any | Propose and check a home page with commands | Needs the same fields, the same check and a machine-readable health report |

**Their visitors, by goal:**

| Goal | Visitors | What they need first |
|---|---|---|
| **Get hired** | Recruiters, hiring managers, interview panels ([README §2](README.md)) | Level, range, proof, the résumé, a way to reach the person |
| **Win clients** | A founder or product lead with a project; an agency looking for a partner | What the person does, for whom, results, how they work, how to start |
| **Share my work** | Readers, peers, editors, event organisers | What it's about, the best pieces, what's new, how to follow or invite |

## 2. The goal

The goal is one choice, made once and changed any time. It sets defaults; every one of them can be changed on its own.

| | Get hired | Win clients | Share my work |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Always-on actions** | Read résumé, Contact me | See the work, Start a project | Read the latest, Get in touch |
| **Question set** (the Questions view) | The nine hiring questions ([questions](questions.md)) | What do you do, for whom, what came of it, how do you work, how do we start | What's this about, where do I start, what's new, who are you |
| **Lens presets** | By role, named by the person (for example: a leadership role, a hands-on role) | By service or by industry | By theme |
| **Proof that counts most** | Shipped, led, said | Shipped, said (clients' words), recognised | Wrote, spoke, built |
| **Default view** | Bento | Bento | Cover |
| **The contact page's label** | Contact me | Start a project | Get in touch |

- **Read résumé appears only when the site names its résumé page,** a new optional site setting `resumePage`, like `contactPage` ([schema.ts](https://github.com/prabinpebam/atiya/blob/main/src/site/content/schema.ts)). Without it, the first action is the goal's second one.
- **The positioning line** under the name is the site setting `positioning`, which already exists; the home page never invents one.
- **A goal is not a template:** it changes no colours, fonts or sections. Those are themes and the site's structure.

## 3. Strengths and their proof

### 3.1 A strength

| Part | What | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Name | What it is: "Design leadership", "Brand identity", "Long-form writing" | About 4 words |
| Short name | For the tight places: the phone's index tile, chips, the chapter bar | 12 characters |
| Line | Its proof, as a fact: "Handoff is now code", not "Passionate about AI" | 6 words |
| What it matches | Topics, sections and words that find its proof ([curation §4](curation.md)) | Any |
| Proof | Pinned items, then what the rules find | Ranked; budgets per level (§6) |
| Voice | A colleague's or client's words, word for word from a recommendation | 12 words |
| Picture | A picture of its own, or the best proof's lead picture | One, with its dark version |

**Starting points.** A person who doesn't know what to call their strengths gets three ways in, none of them required ([owner flows §2](owner-flows.md)):
- **Suggest from my pages:** the rules (or AI, where it's set up) read the site and propose strengths with the pages behind each.
- **Common strengths:** a short list per field (design, engineering, product, research, writing, and others), each a starting name to edit. The list is the engine's, in a catalogue like the services' (`src/site/home/catalogue.json`, beside [`src/site/services/catalogue.json`](https://github.com/prabinpebam/atiya/blob/main/src/site/services/catalogue.json)), never content.
- **Write my own.**

### 3.2 Why three to six

- **Fewer than three** isn't a range: a home of tiles with one or two in it reads as padding. With 0 to 2 strengths, the home stays the Classic hub, with the positioning line.
- **More than six** can't be read on a phone's first screen at a readable size (Bento's index tile, Cover's lines, the Showreel's chapter bar all hold six short names), so the coverage rule ([foundation §5](foundation.md)) would fail. A seventh is refused with a note: merge two, or make one a topic.
- **Fewer is easier to remember.** Working memory holds about four chunks ([Cowan, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X01003922)); the recall target is 4 of 6 for that reason. Four or five strengths is the sweet spot, and edit mode says so; six is allowed because a wide range is the very thing the owner's site has to show.

### 3.3 Kinds of proof

The owner's five kinds, plus two that other sites need.

| Kind | Means | Usually from |
|---|---|---|
| `shipped` | Released, in use, with an outcome | A case study, a project page |
| `led` | Led a team, a program or a change | A case study, a résumé line |
| `built` | Made it personally | A project page, a gallery |
| `wrote` | Wrote about it | An article |
| `spoke` (new) | Talked about it | A talk page (`kind: talk`) |
| `recognised` (new) | An award, a publication, a certification | A page, a résumé line |
| `said` | Someone else's words | A recommendation or a client's testimonial |

The Proof grid shows at most five kinds as columns: the five the site uses most. How each page gets a kind is [curation §3](curation.md).

### 3.4 Where proof comes from

| Source | Written as | The check |
|---|---|---|
| A page | `ref:article/<id>` | Published and open (status `published`, visibility `public`) |
| A part of a page | `ref:article/<id>#<key>` | The block exists. Needs block keys ([AI editing §3](../editor/ai-editing.md#3-blocks-need-a-stable-name)) |
| A recommendation | `recommendation: <id>` | It's in `recommendations.json` ([recommendations](../sections/recommendations.md)) |
| A résumé line | `resume: "<fragment>"` | The fragment is word for word in the `resumePage` |
| A fact | `facts[]` with its source | The same as the source it names |

A private page is never proof, the same rule as every way to explore ([navigation modes](../desktop/modes.md), V25). Work that can't be public is a résumé line with **Ask me about it** ([foundation §4](foundation.md)).

## 4. Every view, at three to six strengths

Every view was designed at six. Here's what each does with fewer, so a site with four strengths doesn't look like a six-strength site with holes.

| View | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 (as designed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Bento](bento.md) | One row of three tall tiles; each shows two proofs at L2 | Two by two, six columns each | Two wide tiles (the first two in order), then three | Two rows of three |
| [Cover](cover.md) | Three cover lines, larger, each with its line | Four lines | Five lines | Six numbered lines |
| [Spotlight](spotlight.md) | Jump to holds three buttons; the rail keeps every highlight | Four buttons | Five buttons | Six buttons, eight highlights |
| [Showreel](showreel.md) | Five chapters, about 60 seconds | Six, about 70 seconds | Seven, about 80 seconds | Eight chapters, about 90 seconds |
| [Keywords](keywords.md) | Three groups, up to 8 keywords each | Four groups | Five groups | Six groups |
| [Proof grid](proof-grid.md) | Three rows; the extra height goes to bigger cells | Four rows | Five rows | Six rows |
| [Questions](questions.md) | Question 1's answer lists three strengths | Four | Five | Six |

On a phone, every view stacks as it does at six, with fewer items. The coverage test runs each offered view at the site's own count, so it holds for every site, not only the owner's.

## 5. When a view can be offered

A view below its minimum isn't offered: it's left out of Change view, and edit mode says what it needs ("Spotlight needs 4 highlights with pictures; you have 2"). A view is never shown half-empty.

| View | Needs | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Bento | 3 strengths, each with at least one proof | The tiles are the strengths |
| Cover | The above, and a picture for the cover | A cover without a picture is a list |
| Spotlight | 4 highlights, each with a picture | With fewer, the rail is too short to browse, and the stage is the whole view |
| Showreel | 5 highlights with pictures, and every strength with a picture | Every chapter needs something to move |
| Keywords | 12 keywords and 8 proof items | Fewer, and every pick returns everything |
| Proof grid | 3 kinds of proof in use, and 8 proof items | Otherwise the grid is mostly empty cells |
| Questions | 5 questions answered | Fewer reads as a FAQ with gaps |
| Classic hub | Nothing | Always available, and the fallback |

The default view can't be one below its minimum; if the default falls below it (a page made private, a picture removed), the site uses Bento, or the Classic hub, and the health report says why ([curation §8](curation.md)).

**As built:** the content check doesn't refuse a view below its minimum, because a page made private elsewhere would then stop the whole site. The site falls back as above (`effectiveViews` in `src/site/home/health.ts`), and edit mode's Home screen refuses to save such a view as the default.

## 6. Thin sites and big sites

The home page has **fixed budgets per level**, so a site with 500 pages has the same calm first screen as a site with five.

| Level | Budget | A thin site (5 pages) | A big site (500 pages) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Every strength's name, the positioning line, two actions | The same | The same |
| L2 | One proof line, one picture, one voice per strength | Some strengths have no voice or picture: the tile says less, never pads | The best of many, by score ([curation §5](curation.md)) |
| L3 | The top 5 proof items per strength (3 to 8, a setting) | Often fewer than 5 | The top 5, then **Show all 23**, which expands inside the drawer |
| Beyond | The pages themselves | | A strength's full list is the drawer expanded, with no new page type |

The highlights (Spotlight, Showreel) keep their own budget of at most 8, picked as [curation §6](curation.md) says.

## 7. What's whose

| Lives in | What | Who changes it |
|---|---|---|
| **The engine** (`src/site/home/`) | The views, the rules, the catalogue of goals, common strengths and question sets, the minimums and budgets | A new engine release |
| **The site's content** (`content/showcase.json`, `structures/ways.json`) | The goal, the strengths and their lines, pins, hides, voices, lenses, tailored links, view settings, the default and offered views | The person, through edit mode or an agent |
| **Computed** (never stored) | The picks: which proof, which highlights, in which order | Recomputed at each build and live in edit mode |
| **The owner's only** | His six strengths, their words, his lenses | Him |

Home views are an **engine feature from the start**, with the owner's site as its first user, not a feature of his site made generic later. Building it generic costs little more (the catalogue and the 3-to-6 layouts), and it's the job J5 already promises: "choose how visitors explore it".

## 8. A new site

Setup's three choices don't change (promise P2, [desktop spec §1](../desktop/spec.md#1-the-customer-promise)).

1. A new site starts on the **Classic hub**, as today.
2. Once it has **three published pages**, edit mode's Dashboard and the Pages screen show a card, **Make your home page**, with what it takes (about ten minutes, five with AI) and Not now.
3. The Home screen's first run asks for the goal, then strengths, then checks the proof ([owner flows §2](owner-flows.md)). The home page changes only when the person chooses a default view.
4. An agent does the same with `atiya home` commands ([AI curation §8](ai-curation.md)).

The starter's sample pages carry topics and kinds, so a person who keeps them sees a working home page before writing a word, with the samples' words marked as samples.

## 9. Decisions

| # | Decision | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| FS1 | The word owners see | Strengths; capabilities; skills | Strengths: a person's own word; "skills" sounds like a list of tools |
| FS2 | The goals | Three (hired, clients, share); free-form; none | Three, each a set of defaults, with every default changeable |
| FS3 | The range of strengths | 3 to 6; 2 to 8; any number | 3 to 6, held by the coverage rule |
| FS4 | New kinds of proof | `spoke` and `recognised`; free-form kinds | The two, so the Proof grid's columns stay meaningful |
| FS5 | Home views for every site | From the start, the owner's site first; the owner's only, generic later | From the start (README H10) |
| FS6 | When "Make your home page" appears | At 3 published pages; at once; never, only from the Home screen | At 3 pages: before that, the hub is the honest home |
