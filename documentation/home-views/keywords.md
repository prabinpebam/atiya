# Keywords

Keywords lets the visitor say what they hire for. A field of keywords floats gently in six groups, one per capability, sized by how much evidence stands behind each. The visitor picks the ones in the role, and the matching work comes forward, best first, each result saying why it matched. It turns a recruiter's job description into a filter over the whole site. It's part of [Home page views](README.md) and reads the [capability spine](foundation.md).

> **TL;DR.**
> - **Pick what you hire for:** keywords in six labelled groups (L1: the group labels are the six capabilities); a bigger keyword has more evidence behind it.
> - **The matches are the signal (L2):** ranked, each with its proof line and "Match: Agentic AI". **Opening a match** shows its evidence (L3).
> - **Nothing picked shows all six capabilities,** so a visitor who never picks still sees every one. Typing a skill works too, with synonyms, and says honestly when there's no evidence yet.
> - **The picks travel:** Copy link keeps them, so a recruiter can send a hiring manager the same filtered page; Ask about these opens the contact form with the picks as its topic.
> - **The field drifts only in a view the visitor chose,** pauses under the pointer, and is still under reduced motion.

## 1. The idea

Recruiters screen against a list: the job description's skills. Every other view asks them to translate that list into the owner's own words; Keywords takes their words and does the translating. A floating cloud makes it inviting, the sizes show where the depth is, and picking turns a passive read into a small conversation ("show me product strategy and agentic AI"). The cost is that it asks the visitor to do something, so it guards the failure case on its first screen: the six capabilities are named by the groups and shown in the results before any pick.

## 2. Who it serves

| Persona | How Keywords serves them |
|---|---|
| Recruiter | Their job description's words, matched against evidence in seconds; a link to send on with the picks in it |
| Hiring manager | Arriving from a recruiter's link, the profile already filtered to the role |
| Panel member | Each result says what it matched and what kind of evidence it is |
| General audience | A playful, visual way in; picking feels like a game |
| Returning visitor | The same picks from the same link |

## 3. The three levels

| Level | In Keywords | Revealed by |
|---|---|---|
| **L1, the map** | The six group labels; with nothing picked, the six capability summaries in the results | Nothing |
| **L2, the signal** | Keyword sizes (the depth of evidence); each match's title, proof line, match reason and kind | A glance; a pick |
| **L3, the detail** | Opening a match: its evidence items (Bento's drawer, in the results column) | Pressing a match |
| **Beyond** | The article, the résumé | Links in the evidence |

## 5. Interaction

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Press a keyword | It's picked: it fills, a copy joins the tray, the matches update; press again to unpick |
| Remove a chip in the tray; Clear all | Unpicks it; unpicks everything, and the matches go back to the six capabilities |
| Type in "Or type a skill" | Suggests keywords as you type, through synonyms ("GenAI" suggests Agentic AI; "design ops", Operating models); Enter picks the suggestion |
| Type a skill with no evidence | "Nothing on the site shows Kotlin yet." and Ask me about it, which opens the contact form with the skill as its topic |
| Press a match | Its evidence opens in place, in the matches column |
| Copy link | Copies `/views/keywords/?pick=product-strategy,agentic-ai`; opening it restores the picks |
| Ask about these | The contact form, with the picks as its topic |
| Pointer over the field, or focus in it | The drift pauses |

## 6. Task flows

**Recruiter with a job description** (about 1 minute)
1. Picks Keywords from Change view. Picks Agentic AI, Product strategy and Hiring.
2. Reads the top matches: AI transformation, Microsoft Family, Design leadership.
3. Types "design ops": picks Operating models from the suggestion.
4. Presses Copy link and pastes it in an email to the hiring manager, with Read résumé's PDF.

**Hiring manager from the recruiter's link**
1. Lands with the four picks in the tray and the matches ranked. Reads the first two.
2. Opens AI transformation: the AI program (Ask me about it), Cloner Pro, the essay.
3. Presses Ask about these: the contact form, topic filled.

**Recruiter for a skill the site doesn't show**
1. Types "Kotlin". Reads "Nothing on the site shows Kotlin yet." Presses Ask me about it.

**General visitor**
1. Plays with the field, picks 3D and Storytelling, opens My journey with DesignUp.

## 7. Visual direction

- **A field, not a tag cloud:** keywords are pill chips in clusters, each cluster under its capability's name in Figtree's label style, with air between clusters so the six read as six.
- **Size is information:** three sizes from the evidence; the largest keywords in Fraunces, the others in Figtree, so the depth shows at a glance.
- **Picked is the spot colour:** a picked chip fills with indigo, and its copy in the tray matches; nothing else on the page is filled.
- **A drift so slight it reads as alive:** a few pixels over ten seconds or so, each cluster at its own pace, never enough to make a keyword hard to press.

## 8. Motion

| Motion | When | Duration | Reduced motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| The field drifts | Only in a view the visitor chose (Change view, or the stored choice); paused under the pointer, with focus in the field, or with the tab hidden | Continuous, slow | Still |
| A picked keyword travels to the tray | A pick | About 300 ms | Appears in the tray |
| The matches reorder | A pick or unpick | About 300 ms (each card slides to its place) | Instant |
| A match's evidence opens | Pressing a match | About 250 ms | Instant |

The drift is motion on its own, so it falls under the same amendment as the Showreel (open decision H4 in [Home page views](README.md)): only in a view the visitor chose, paused on contact, still under reduced motion, and with a **Stop the drift** toggle under the field.

## 9. Accessibility

- Each group is a `role="group"` named by its capability; each keyword is a toggle button with `aria-pressed`.
- The field uses a roving tabindex: Tab enters it once, the arrow keys move between keywords (across groups), Tab leaves it for the matches. Each group's first keyword is reachable from its label.
- A polite live region says what changed: "Agentic AI picked. 3 matches."
- Picking never depends on dragging (WCAG 2.5.7): pressing picks. Dragging a keyword to the tray is an extra, not the way.
- The drift never moves a keyword under the pointer, and stops while the pointer or focus is in the field, so targets don't move as you aim.
- "Or type a skill" is a combobox with a listbox of suggestions; with no suggestion, it says so in text, not only by an empty list.

## 10. What it needs

| Need | Detail |
|---|---|
| Content | A `keywords` block in the spine: each keyword's id, label, capability, synonyms, and the proof items it matches; the owner curates it against real job descriptions |
| Components | A Keywords layout; a keyword field (toggle chips in groups), a tray, a match list reusing Bento's drawer; the skill combobox |
| Index | Built at build time into the page: keywords, synonyms and matches. No server and no external search |
| Tests | Coverage at 1280 and 360 (the group labels, and the six summaries with nothing picked); picking by keyboard; `?pick=` restores; the no-evidence path; drift stops under reduced motion and on hover; axe; screenshots |
| Telemetry | `keyword_pick` (`id`, `on`), `skill_typed` (`matched`: true or false only, never the text), plus the shared events |
| On every site | 3 to 6 groups, one per strength, up to 8 keywords each ([for every site §4](for-every-site.md)). The keywords are each strength's `keywords`, offered from the topic vocabulary's labels and synonyms ([curation §4.1](curation.md)) for the person to edit. Offered with 12 keywords and 8 proof items ([§5](for-every-site.md)). Settings: Motion, Order, Typing a skill and Per group ([controls §3](controls.md)) |

## 11. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The visitor never picks, and so never sees the depth | With nothing picked, the matches are the six capability summaries; the group labels name them; the sizes show depth without a pick |
| The keywords miss the recruiter's words | Synonyms in "Or type a skill"; the owner checks the set against ten real job descriptions for the roles he wants; `skill_typed` with `matched: false` shows how often it misses |
| "Nothing on the site shows it yet" looks like a weakness | It's honest, and it turns into a conversation with Ask me about it |
| A tag cloud looks dated | Clusters, three sizes and a slight drift, not a jumble of sizes and angles |
| Motion makes targets hard to press | The drift stops on contact; it's a few pixels |

## 12. Open decisions

| # | Decision | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | The drift | Drift; still | Drift, slight, only in a chosen view, with Stop the drift |
| K2 | Free typing | Keywords only; typing with synonyms | Typing with synonyms, and the honest no-evidence answer |
| K3 | Picks in the link | Yes; no | Yes: a recruiter's filtered link is the view's best use |
| K4 | Matching two picks | A match for any pick; only for all | Any, ranked by how many it matches |
