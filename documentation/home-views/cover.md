# Cover

Cover presents the home page as a magazine cover: a masthead with the name and the positioning, one strong cover picture, a cover story, and six numbered cover lines, one per capability, each with its proof. Scrolling opens the cover into a contents spread. It gives the strongest editorial first impression for the least work, and suits a site whose type and pictures already read like a magazine. It's part of [Home page views](README.md) and reads the [capability spine](foundation.md).

> **TL;DR.**
> - **A cover that is also a table of contents:** the masthead, a cover picture and the cover story (Defining Microsoft Family) on the left; **In this issue**, the six capabilities as numbered cover lines with their proof, on the right (L1 and L2 on one screen).
> - **Scrolling turns the page:** the cover settles into a contents spread, where each cover line becomes a short feature with its evidence (L3).
> - **On a phone,** the cover is about square, so the six numbered cover lines still fit on the first screen under it.
> - **Low effort, high impact:** it uses pictures and type the site already has, and little script.

## 1. The idea

A magazine cover has a second or two on a news-stand to make someone pick it up, and it does it with a strong picture, a name, and a few cover lines that promise what's inside. The home page has the same second or two. Cover lines are made to be scanned, and the numbered list ("01 Leadership") says there are six things here, and what they are, before the visitor reads any one of them. The site's visual language is already editorial (Fraunces, Newsreader, the paper and ink palette), so Cover is the view that looks most like the rest of the site.

## 2. Who it serves

| Persona | How Cover serves them |
|---|---|
| Recruiter | The name, the positioning and six numbered capabilities in one glance; résumé and contact in the strip |
| Hiring manager | A confident, curated first impression; the cover story leads with real product strategy work |
| Panel member | The contents spread gives each capability a short feature with its evidence |
| General audience | The most beautiful first screen; the cover picture and the cover story invite reading |
| Returning visitor | "This issue" can change: the cover story can be swapped for new work without changing the layout |

## 3. The three levels

| Level | In Cover | Revealed by |
|---|---|---|
| **L1, the map** | The masthead (name, positioning) and the six numbered cover lines' names | Nothing: the cover |
| **L2, the signal** | Each cover line's proof ("01 Leadership: Built Belong's design org"); the cover story's picture and line | A glance |
| **L3, the detail** | The contents spread: per capability, a short feature (a picture, two lines, three to five evidence items) | Scrolling, or pressing a cover line, which scrolls to its feature |
| **Beyond** | The cover story's article; each item's page | Links in the features |

## 4. Layout and composition

### 4.1 Wide screen (1280 px)

**The cover**, the first screen, two columns at about 60 and 40:
- **Left, the cover:** the masthead, "Prabin Pebam", in Fraunces at the display size, with the positioning under it; the cover picture, large, about 360 by 250 at the reference width (a portrait or the Family app, chosen by the owner); the cover story box over the picture's lower edge: "Defining Microsoft Family", one line ("From a 2020 app to a 2026 Windows commitment to families"), Read the story.
- **Right, In this issue:** the six cover lines, numbered 01 to 06, each the capability's short name in Fraunces and its proof line in Newsreader, separated by hairlines; at the foot, "Also: résumé, contact, voices".
- The strip above, as in every view.

**The contents spread**, after one scroll:
- The six cover lines become six features in a two-column magazine layout, each with its number large in the margin, a picture, its two lines and its evidence as a short list.
- Between features 03 and 04, a pull quote from a recommendation, set large.
- After 06: the site's sections as the "departments", and Other ways to explore.

### 4.2 Tablet (768 px)

The cover goes full width, at 4:3; In this issue sits under it as a two-column list of the six cover lines, still within the first screen.

### 4.3 Phone (360 px)

| Order | Content | Height (about) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The strip: name and Menu | 40 px |
| 2 | The cover, about square: the masthead with a short positioning ("Leader · Strategy · AI"), the cover picture, the cover story as a band at its foot | 360 px |
| 3 | In this issue: the six numbered lines, one per row, each a link to its feature | 272 px |

That's about 670 px, so the six cover lines are on the first screen of a 740 px tall phone. A magazine's 4:5 cover would push them below it, so the phone trades the classic proportion for coverage.

## 5. Interaction

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Press a cover line | Scrolls to its feature in the contents spread, which takes focus |
| Scroll down from the cover | The cover picture eases back and the contents spread comes up under it (a scroll-driven effect) |
| Read the story | Opens the cover story's article |
| Press a feature's evidence item | Opens its page, or Ask me about it for a résumé line |

## 6. Task flows

**Recruiter** (about 15 seconds)
1. Sees the name, the positioning and the six numbered lines.
2. Presses Read résumé in the strip.

**Hiring manager**
1. Takes in the cover; reads In this issue top to bottom (about 10 seconds).
2. Presses "03 AI": the page scrolls to the AI feature, with its evidence.
3. Reads the cover story; comes back; presses Contact me.

**General visitor**
1. Drawn in by the cover picture, opens the cover story.

## 7. Visual direction

- **A real cover's hierarchy:** one dominant picture; the name as a masthead; cover lines set in two sizes (the number and name, then the proof line).
- **Restraint:** the paper background, ink type, the indigo spot only on the cover story's band and the numbers.
- **The picture carries the impression.** It must be the best picture on the site, in light and dark versions; a portrait drawn as an ink sketch is an option that keeps it in the site's illustration language.
- **The issue changes:** the cover story and picture can be swapped (a spine highlight marked `cover`) without changing anything else, which gives returning visitors something new.

## 8. Motion

| Motion | When | Reduced motion |
|---|---|---|
| The cover eases back as the spread scrolls in (scroll-driven, `animation-timeline: scroll()`) | Scrolling | None: a plain scroll |
| Scroll to a feature | Pressing a cover line | Instant jump |

Both answer the visitor's own scroll or press, so neither needs an exception to [visual language §6](../site-ui/visual-language.md).

## 9. Accessibility

- The masthead is the page's H1; In this issue is a navigation landmark named "In this issue" with an ordered list of links; each feature is a section with an H2 matching its cover line.
- The cover picture's alt text describes it; the cover story is a link with its title as its name.
- Contrast holds over the picture: the cover story band and the phone strapline sit on solid paper or spot fills, never directly on the picture.

## 10. What it needs

| Need | Detail |
|---|---|
| Content | The spine; a highlight marked as the cover story; the cover picture, light and dark |
| Components | A Cover layout (masthead, picture, story band, contents list) and a Feature compound for the spread; both from existing fundamentals |
| Tests | Coverage at 1280 and 360; axe; screenshots in both modes at three widths |
| On every site | 3 to 6 cover lines, larger with fewer ([for every site §4](for-every-site.md)). Offered with Bento's minimum and a cover picture ([§5](for-every-site.md)). The default view for Share my work. Settings: Density, Order, Cover picture and Cover story ([controls §3](controls.md)) |

## 11. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A beautiful cover the visitor doesn't read past | In this issue is on the cover, not after it; every cover line is a link |
| The cover story biases the visitor toward one capability | It's tagged and labelled as one story among six; the lens can swap it for the role's capability |
| The phone's cover pushes In this issue below the fold | It's about square on a phone, not 4:5, so the six lines fit on the first screen |
| A weak cover picture | The owner picks it; the default is an ink sketch made for it |

## 12. Open decisions

| # | Decision | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | The cover picture | A portrait; the Family app; an ink sketch | An ink sketch portrait: it's the site's own language and works in light and dark |
| C2 | The cover story | Defining Microsoft Family; the lens's capability's highlight; rotated per visit | Defining Microsoft Family, swapped by the lens; never rotated per visit |
| C3 | The contents spread | Full features; a compact list | Full features: Cover is the view for visitors who read |

## 13. As built (stage 4)

Cover is built and offered at `/views/cover/`. It's drawn from the spine by [curation](curation.md)'s rules, so it shows whatever strengths a site has, 3 to 6, numbered in the issue's order.

| Part | Where |
|---|---|
| The view | [`Cover.astro`](https://github.com/prabinpebam/atiya/blob/main/src/site/components/compounds/Cover.astro), with the lead and [`Voices.astro`](https://github.com/prabinpebam/atiya/blob/main/src/site/components/compounds/Voices.astro) in its slots; its story is in the design library |
| From the spine to the issue | [`cover.ts`](https://github.com/prabinpebam/atiya/blob/main/src/site/home/cover.ts) (pure, unit-tested in `homeCover.test.ts`): the cover lines, the cover story and the cover picture, joined to the site's pages and pictures by [`homeView.ts`](https://github.com/prabinpebam/atiya/blob/main/src/site/content/homeView.ts) |
| Its sizes | The `c.cover` tokens: the cover picture, a feature's picture and number, and the container queries it lays itself out by |

Where the build differs from the design above:

- **The lead is the masthead.** The person's name is the page's H1, with their role, their facts and the actions (Read résumé, Contact me, Explore the planet, Change view) in a plain box, the same lead every view has ([foundation §7](foundation.md)), rather than a masthead over the picture.
