# Controls

How much a person can change about their home page, and how that's arranged so most people need only the first five minutes and nobody is short of power. The controls live on edit mode's Home screen ([Making your home page](owner-flows.md)); what they decide is stored as described in [Curating the home page](curation.md). It's part of [Home page views](README.md).

> **TL;DR.**
> - **Three layers:**
>   - **Essentials:** the goal, 3 to 6 strengths and the default view. Enough for a good home page.
>   - **Tune**, when something looks wrong: one tab each for strengths, highlights, views and links.
>   - **Advanced, behind one More options:** matching, recency, holding changes, how much detail and the topic vocabulary. Most people never open it.
>   - Nothing is more than two levels deep.
> - **Every item has three states:** Auto (the rules decide), Pinned (always shown, first) and Hidden (never shown). Every Auto pick says why.
> - **Content once, presentation per view:** what's said (strengths, lines, proof, voices) is shared by every view. Each view's settings come from one vocabulary of five (Lead, Density, Pictures, Motion, Order) plus at most two of its own, and no view shows more than five settings.
> - **Guardrails, not warnings:** a 7th strength, a line of 7 words, a default view below its minimum or a Showreel that plays on arrival can't be saved; each refusal says why and what to do instead.
> - **Only differences are stored,** so a better default in a new engine release reaches every site that never changed it.

## 1. Three layers

Progressive disclosure: the few things everyone needs first, the rest on request, and never more than two levels, past which people lose their way between them ([Nielsen Norman Group, progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/)). Level one is the Home screen's tabs; level two is a row opened in place or More options. There's no third.

**Essentials** (the first run asks for exactly these, [owner flows §2](owner-flows.md)):

| Control | Values | Default | Stored as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goal | Get hired, Win clients, Share my work | Asked | `showcase.json` `goal` |
| Strengths | 3 to 6, each a name and a short name | Asked | `capabilities[]` |
| The default view | Any view that meets its minimum | The goal's ([for every site §2](for-every-site.md)) | `ways.json` `home.default` |

**Tune** (one tab each on the Home screen):

| Tab | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Strengths** | **Who you are** first: the positioning line (the site setting), facts with their sources, the labels of the two always-on actions. Then, per strength: name, short name, line, voice, picture, what it matches, and its proof in three states (§4). Order, by drag or Move up and Move down |
| **Highlights** | Pin, hide and order; each highlight's strength |
| **Views** | Offer, Default, and each view's settings (§3) |
| **Links** | The person's lenses and links for one job ([owner flows §5](owner-flows.md)) |

## 2. Advanced: More options

At the foot of the Home screen's left pane, closed by default. Each setting says in one line what it does and shows its effect in the preview. All but Topics are stored in `showcase.json` `curation` ([curation §2](curation.md)); Topics is `content/topics.json`.

| Setting | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **How strict** | Loose, Balanced, Strict | Balanced | How closely a page must match a strength to count as its proof: the matching threshold 0.3, 0.4 or 0.7 ([curation §4](curation.md)) |
| **Prefer recent work** | Off, Some, Strong | Some | How much newer proof outranks older: no decay, a 3-year half-life or a 1-year one ([curation §5](curation.md)) |
| **Details shown** | 3 to 8 | 5 | How many proof items a strength's details show before Show all |
| **Leave out** | Sections, topics, kinds of page | None | Never used as proof anywhere: an archive section, personal notes, the starter's samples |
| **Hold automatic changes** | Off, On | Off | Freezes the picks as they are; new pages wait in a list with Release ([curation §7](curation.md)) |
| **Topics** | The vocabulary: each topic's label and synonyms; Merge; Rename | Made from the topics in use | What matching reads ([curation §4.1](curation.md)) |

The weights of matching and scoring are the engine's, not settings: a person can't judge a weight, but can judge a result, which Pin and Hide fix directly.

## 3. View settings: one vocabulary

What a view **says** is the person's content, shared by every view, so changing a line changes it everywhere and a view can never contradict another. How a view **shows** it is that view's settings, drawn from five shared ones, so learning one view's settings teaches them all.

| Setting | Values | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | Who you are, A highlight, A voice | What the first and largest place shows |
| **Density** | Calm, Full | How much shows at rest; Full brings forward what Calm leaves for a hover, a press or the details |
| **Pictures** | From the work, Chosen only, Type only | From the work: a strength's own picture, else its proof's. Chosen only: only pictures the person chose for strengths, for a consistent set (like ink sketches). Type only: none |
| **Motion** | Still, Gentle, Lively | Still: changes are instant. Gentle: motion only in answer to the visitor. Lively: the view's own motion as well. Reduced motion on the visitor's device always means Still |
| **Order** | Yours, Strongest first | The order of strengths or highlights: the person's, or by the depth of their proof. A lens overrides both |

**Each view uses the shared settings that apply, and at most two of its own, five in all:**

| View | Shared | Its own | Defaults |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Bento](bento.md) | Lead, Density, Pictures, Order | **In their words:** On, Off | Who you are, Calm, From the work, Yours, On |
| [Cover](cover.md) | Density (Full: features; Calm: a list), Order | **Cover picture**; **Cover story:** a highlight | Full, Yours |
| [Spotlight](spotlight.md) | Motion, Order | **Highlights:** 4 to 8; **The rail:** Titles, Thumbnails | Gentle, Yours, 8, Titles |
| [Showreel](showreel.md) | Order | **Pace:** Slower, Standard; **The film:** Offer the download, Don't | Yours, Standard, Offer |
| [Keywords](keywords.md) | Motion (Lively: the drift), Order | **Typing a skill:** On, Off; **Per group:** 4 to 8 keywords | Lively, Yours, On, 8 |
| [Proof grid](proof-grid.md) | Density (Full: counts; Calm: marks), Order | **Columns:** which five kinds; **Empty cells:** Show, Hide | Full, Yours, the five most used, Show |
| [Questions](questions.md) | Order | **Questions:** the goal's set, edited; **Ask something else:** On, Off | Yours, the goal's, On |

**Three views need words of their own,** because their form is words: Questions (an answer per question), Showreel (a caption per chapter) and Keywords (each strength's keywords). They're content, not settings: kept in the spine (`questions`, `reel`, `capabilities[].keywords`), edited under the view on the Views tab beside its settings, with Suggest ([AI curation §4](ai-curation.md), jobs 8 and 10). They're held to the rules every word on the home page is held to: made from the strengths and their proof, each citing its source, quotes word for word ([AI curation §3](ai-curation.md)). So they add to what the other views say, and can't contradict it. Without them, Showreel's captions are the highlights' titles and the strengths' lines; Questions isn't offered until it has five answers ([for every site §5](for-every-site.md)).

**Stored** under the view in `content/structures/ways.json`, beside the offered views ([switcher §5](switcher.md)), and only where it differs from the default:

```json
{
  "home": {
    "default": "bento",
    "offered": ["bento", "cover", "proof-grid"],
    "settings": { "bento": { "density": "full" }, "cover": { "story": "family" } }
  }
}
```

## 4. Auto, Pinned, Hidden

Every item the rules can place (a proof item under a strength, a highlight, a keyword) is in one of three states.

| State | Means | Shown in edit mode as |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto** | The rules decide whether and where it appears | Its reason: "Auto: topic AI, a case study, 2026" |
| **Pinned** | Always shown, before every Auto item, in the pins' own order | "Pinned by you", with its place |
| **Hidden** | Never shown here | Folded at the end: "3 hidden · Show" |

- **Changing a state** is one menu on the row (Auto, Pin, Hide), with a keyboard twin, and one step in undo.
- **Hidden is per strength:** a page hidden from AI can still prove Making. **Hide from home**, in the article's settings, hides it everywhere.
- **Voices have no Auto:** a rule never chooses someone's words ([curation §10](curation.md), CU6).
- **More pins than places** is allowed and said: "6 pinned, 5 shown: the first 5. Change Details shown or unpin one."
- **Pins survive change:** a pinned page renamed or moved stays pinned (IDs never change); a pinned page made private, unpublished or deleted loses its pin in the same step, and that step says so ([curation §7](curation.md)).

## 5. Guardrails

Things that would break the home page can't be saved. Each refusal says what and what to do instead, in the field.

| Rule | Why | What the person sees |
|---|---|---|
| 3 to 6 strengths | The coverage rule ([for every site §3.2](for-every-site.md)) | Add strength disabled at 6: "Six is the most a phone's first screen can show. Merge two, or make one a topic." Below 3 visible, the home is Classic's hub, and hiding the third asks first |
| A short name, 12 characters or fewer | It's what tight places show | The field's count and a refusal past 12 |
| A line of 6 words or fewer; a voice of 12 or fewer, word for word | The copy rules ([foundation §8](foundation.md)) | A count of words; a voice is chosen by selecting a sentence, never typed |
| No default below its minimum | A view is never shown half-empty ([for every site §5](for-every-site.md)) | The view's Default is disabled, with what it needs |
| A Showreel never plays on arrival | Motion starts only by the visitor's choice (Showreel R3) | As the default, it opens on its first frame with Play |
| The two always-on actions stay | Contact is the conversion | They can be renamed, never removed |
| A lens never hides | The coverage rule holds under every lens ([foundation §6](foundation.md)) | A lens has an order and a lead voice, and no Hide |
| No colours, fonts or spacing per view | That's the site's theme ([theming](../site-ui/theming.md)) | Views have no such settings; the Views tab links to the themes panel |

## 6. The same fields for agents

Every control is a field in `content/showcase.json` or `content/structures/ways.json`, held by the content contract. An agent changes them the way edit mode does: through a proposal the person reviews, or `atiya home apply` after the same check ([AI curation §8](ai-curation.md)). There's no setting an agent can reach that a person can't see, and none a person can set that an agent can't read.

## 7. Decisions

| # | Decision | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| CT1 | How controls are arranged | Three layers, two levels deep; every control on one screen | Three layers: the first run needs three answers, not thirty |
| CT2 | View settings | One shared vocabulary plus up to two of a view's own; free settings per view | The shared vocabulary, at most five per view |
| CT3 | Where view settings are stored | `structures/ways.json` beside the offered views; `showcase.json` | `ways.json`: presentation with the views, content in the showcase |
| CT4 | Different words per view | Allowed freely; content once | Content once: the views share every line, proof and voice. Questions, Showreel and Keywords add words their form needs, held to the same grounding rules |
| CT5 | Matching weights as settings | Exposed in More options; the engine's | The engine's: How strict, Pin and Hide cover what a person can judge |
| CT6 | What's stored | Only differences from the defaults; every value | Only differences, so better defaults reach every site |
