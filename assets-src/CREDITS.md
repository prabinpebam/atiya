# Asset credits

All 3D geometry in the POC is **original**, built procedurally from three.js primitives (rounded boxes, cones, cylinders, spheres, lathes, tori, extrusions) by the geometry kit in `src/game/world/` and `src/game/player/`. The one exception is the **player characters**: the CC0 Kenney model listed below, with its `skaterMaleA` skin for the Skater and a generated skin plus an original procedural ponytail for Sunny. The **sound effects** are CC0 recordings, also listed below. The **background music** belongs to the site owner, and the **icons** are Font Awesome Free (below). There are no other third-party models, fonts, audio or animations. The ground patterns are a procedural shader, detailed with the generated textures below.

**Generated textures and art (original):** the hand-painted ground tiles (grass, dirt, cobbles, sand, riverbed, rock, the granite boulder mask (2026-09-25), water caustics, a subtle painted brush grain for the buildings), the building surface masks (wood planks, roof shingles, plaster, fieldstone, brick, metal, canvas; 2026-09-25), the tree bark mask (2026-09-25), the painted compass-rose plaza decal (2026-09-25), the top-down, noise-like lawn tile that replaced the blade-stroke grass (2026-09-25), the female character's skin atlas (`characters/casualFemaleA.png`, an image-to-image edit of Kenney's CC0 `skaterMaleA` skin keeping its UV layout; 2026-09-25), the foliage, conifer, pond-plant (lily pads, reeds with cattails, irises, fern), grass and flying-leaf sprites, the moon, and the landing key art and social card were generated for this project with OpenAI **GPT Image 2.5** (`gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` via Azure OpenAI) on 2026-09-24. Sources and the exact prompt for each are in [`textures/`](./textures/) (`<name>.png` + `<name>.prompt.txt`). The only image inputs were this project's own screenshots, used as palette/style references. Tiles were made seamless with the offset-and-inpaint pass of the `gpt-image-2-5` skill. `scripts/build-textures.py` builds the WebP files in `public/textures/`, `public/poster/` and `public/og-image.jpg`. No third-party IP was prompted for or referenced.

**Item icons (original, 2026-09-25):** the 26 inventory icons (log, leaves, apple, orange, stone, and tulip, cosmos and pansy in seven colours) were generated with GPT Image 2.5 by `scripts/gen-icons.py`, image-to-image against a golden style set of four exemplars (a toadstool, a bucket, a crystal and a clover) that was itself generated image-to-image from the landing key art. The flower colours are re-tints of three white-petalled paintings. Sources, prompts and the golden set are in [`icons/`](./icons/) (see its README). The pick-up, pickaxe and leaf-rustle sounds are the existing CC0 sprites below, re-pitched in the engine; nothing new was downloaded.

**Prabin's guitar (2026-09-26):** the strum is synthesised in the browser (Karplus–Strong plucked strings, `src/game/audio/audioLogic.ts`): original, nothing downloaded.

**Crafting icons (original, 2026-09-26):** 10 more icons for the crafting chunk: planks, a wooden beam, a stone slab, and a paint pot in the seven bloom colours. They were made the same way (GPT Image 2.5, `scripts/gen-icons.py`, image-to-image against the same frozen golden set); the seven paints are re-tints of one white-paint painting. The crafting table, Chopper's house and its ghost are original kit geometry. The hammering and sparkle are the existing CC0 sprites (the pickaxe knock and the pick-up pop); nothing new was downloaded.

**Cobblestones (original, 2026-09-26):** the cobble tile (`textures/cobble.png`) was regenerated with GPT Image 2.5 (`tile`, seamless), with this project's own stone surface texture and a crop of a landmark plinth screenshot as palette references; one merged stone was repaired with a masked edit of the same image. `cobble-normal` is derived from it by `scripts/build-textures.py`. Prompts: `textures/cobble.prompt.txt`, `textures/cobble-normal.prompt.txt`.

**Chopper (2026-09-25):** the two photos of Chopper, the owner's Lhasa Apso, were provided by the site owner, Prabin Pebam (`chopper/chopper-photo-1.png`, `chopper-photo-2.png`; all rights reserved), and are shown on his profile card as `public/chopper/chopper-1.webp` and `chopper-2.webp` (built by `scripts/build-textures.py`). His 3D model is original procedural geometry (`src/game/world/chopper/model.ts`). Two images were generated with GPT Image 2.5, image-to-image from those photos and the landing key art: a model sheet used as the proportions and colour reference (`chopper/chopper-model-sheet.png`, not shipped), and the seamless curly-fur tile (`textures/chopper-fur.png` → `public/textures/chopper-fur.webp`). Each has its prompt beside it. His sounds are the CC0 recordings in the table below.

**The family and their home (2026-09-25):** Rojina's, Laija's and Lingjel's skins (`public/models/skins/*.png`) are composed by `assets-src/characters/compose-family.py` from this project's own atlases on Kenney's CC0 layout (Sunny's generated face, the Kenney skater's face cleaned up, flat recoloured clothes with painted chest motifs); their hair and Rojina's glasses are original procedural geometry. They share the CC0 Kenney character model below. The picnic blanket texture (`textures/picnic-mat.png` → `public/textures/picnic-mat.webp`) was generated with GPT Image 2.5 (prompt beside it). The house, campsite, picnic furniture, toys and props are original kit geometry.

**Prabin, the doghouse's inside and the front door (2026-09-26):** Prabin's skin (`public/models/skins/prabin.png`: black hair, a mustard T-shirt with a small drawn pencil, charcoal trousers, brown shoes) is composed the same way by `compose-family.py` from the Kenney skater atlas (CC0). His hammer, the stick for fetch, the birdhouse, the house's hinged door and the room behind it, and Chopper's rebuilt house (its bed, lantern and pixel-letter name plaque) are original kit geometry. So are the yard round the house (2026-09-27): the fence, the vegetable beds, the watering can, the tulsi vrindavan with its diya, the woodpile, the sandals and the broom. The hammer's knock is the existing CC0 stone-step sprite, quieter; nothing new was downloaded.

**Art direction (original, 2026-09-25):** seven game screenshots repainted as concept art with GPT Image 2.5 (image-to-image, the landing key art as a style reference), kept in `documentation/poc-3d-navigation/art-direction/` with their prompts; and four painted cumulus cloud sprites (`assets-src/textures/cloud-a` … `cloud-d`, true alpha, generated image-to-image from those concepts' skies), packed into `public/textures/cloud-atlas.webp`. The `cloud-normal` and `grass-normal` maps are derived by `scripts/build-textures.py` (no generation).

Fonts are the visitor's system fonts (Segoe UI / system-ui); nothing is loaded from a CDN.

**Style reference:** Animal Crossing: New Horizons screenshots (Nookipedia, 2026-09-24) were viewed during development for general style cues only: proportions, bevelled forms, roof, door and window vocabulary, tree and flower shapes, and the tilt-shift look. They are **not** stored in this repository, and no Nintendo characters, logos, buildings, names, text or UI were copied.

**Sound effects:** `python scripts/build-audio.py` downloads the sources above (Freesound's public HQ previews, the OpenGameArt file and the Kenney packs) into the git-ignored `assets-src/audio/.cache/`. It cuts, filters, denoises, levels and loops them, then writes the seven MP3s in `public/audio/` and `src/game/audio/audioManifest.ts`. Each clip was chosen after analysing it (level steadiness, clipping, hum and tonal peaks, spectrograms, loop-seam and per-slot checks); see the spec §4.16. All were checked as CC0 1.0 on their pages on 2026-09-25.
