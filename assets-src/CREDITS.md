# Asset credits

All 3D geometry in the POC is **original**, built procedurally from three.js primitives (rounded boxes, cones, cylinders, spheres, lathes, tori, extrusions) by the geometry kit in `src/game/world/` and `src/game/player/`. The one exception is the **player characters**: the CC0 Kenney model listed below, with its `skaterMaleA` skin for the Skater and a generated skin plus an original procedural ponytail for Sunny. The **sound effects** are CC0 recordings, also listed below. The **background music** belongs to the site owner, and the **icons** are Font Awesome Free (below). The website's pages (not the game) are set in three Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, served from the site (below). There are no other third-party models, fonts, audio or animations. The ground patterns are a procedural shader, detailed with the generated textures below.

**Generated textures and art (original):** the hand-painted ground tiles (grass, dirt, cobbles, sand, riverbed, rock, the granite boulder mask (2026-09-25), water caustics, a subtle painted brush grain for the buildings), the building surface masks (wood planks, roof shingles, plaster, fieldstone, brick, metal, canvas; 2026-09-25), the tree bark mask (2026-09-25), the painted compass-rose plaza decal (2026-09-25), the top-down, noise-like lawn tile that replaced the blade-stroke grass (2026-09-25), the female character's skin atlas (`characters/casualFemaleA.png`, an image-to-image edit of Kenney's CC0 `skaterMaleA` skin keeping its UV layout; 2026-09-25), the foliage, conifer, pond-plant (lily pads, reeds with cattails, irises, fern), grass and flying-leaf sprites, the moon, and the landing key art and social card were generated for this project with OpenAI **GPT Image 2.5** (`gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` via Azure OpenAI) on 2026-09-24. Sources and the exact prompt for each are in [`textures/`](./textures/) (`<name>.png` + `<name>.prompt.txt`). The only image inputs were this project's own screenshots, used as palette/style references. Tiles were made seamless with the offset-and-inpaint pass of the `gpt-image-2-5` skill. `scripts/build-textures.py` builds the WebP files in `public/textures/`, `public/poster/` and `public/og-image.jpg`. No third-party IP was prompted for or referenced.

**Knee-high grass tufts (original, 2026-09-26):** two sheets of four narrow, 3–4-blade meadow tufts each (`textures/meadow-tufts-1.png`, `meadow-tufts-2.png`, with their prompts) were generated with GPT Image 2.5 on a transparent background, sliced into `meadow-tuft-1…8.png` by `scripts/slice-tufts.py`, and packed into the tintable `tuft-atlas` by `scripts/build-textures.py`. They draw the blade grass's knee-high meadows (`documentation/poc-3d-navigation/vegetation/`). The old `grass-card` clump sprite (2026-09-24) is retired: its source stays in `textures/`, but it's no longer built or shipped.

**Item icons (original, 2026-09-25):** the 26 inventory icons (log, leaves, apple, orange, stone, and tulip, cosmos and pansy in seven colours) were generated with GPT Image 2.5 by `scripts/gen-icons.py`, image-to-image against a golden style set of four exemplars (a toadstool, a bucket, a crystal and a clover) that was itself generated image-to-image from the landing key art. The flower colours are re-tints of three white-petalled paintings. Sources, prompts and the golden set are in [`icons/`](./icons/) (see its README). The pick-up, pickaxe and leaf-rustle sounds are the existing CC0 sprites below, re-pitched in the engine; nothing new was downloaded.

**Prabin's guitar (2026-09-26):** the strum is synthesised in the browser (Karplus–Strong plucked strings, `src/game/audio/audioLogic.ts`): original, nothing downloaded.

**Art direction (original, 2026-09-25):** seven game screenshots repainted as concept art with GPT Image 2.5 (image-to-image, the landing key art as a style reference), kept in `documentation/poc-3d-navigation/art-direction/` with their prompts; and four painted cumulus cloud sprites (`assets-src/textures/cloud-a` … `cloud-d`, true alpha, generated image-to-image from those concepts' skies), packed into `public/textures/cloud-atlas.webp`. They are **not** stored in this repository, and no Nintendo characters, logos, buildings, names, text or UI were copied.

**Sound effects:** `python scripts/build-audio.py` downloads the sources above (Freesound's public HQ previews, the OpenGameArt file and the Kenney packs) into the git-ignored `assets-src/audio/.cache/`. It cuts, filters, denoises, levels and loops them, then writes the ten MP3s in `public/audio/` (seven fetched on Start with sound on, and the night's three, fetched when night first falls) and `src/game/audio/audioManifest.ts`. Each clip was chosen after analysing it (level steadiness, clipping, hum and tonal peaks, spectrograms, loop-seam and per-slot checks); see the spec §4.16. All were checked as CC0 1.0 on their pages on 2026-09-25 (the night's on 2026-09-27).
