Create one original editorial illustration. Black ink on clean white paper, light mode.

REFERENCE ROLES: The FIRST reference is the house style anchor. Match its pen work, hatching density, creature design language, level of detail and humour exactly, but NOT its characters or its scene: never draw its explorer, its stage or its books unless the cast below asks for them. Never draw their bird, tree, cliff or exact animals.

CAST (keep these characters recognisable): THE GUIDE, the recurring hero: a small, round, fuzzy creature with huge, slightly mismatched round eyes, a knitted bobble hat with a long floppy tassel, a big coil of rope slung over one shoulder, a whistle on a string round the neck and a jangle of carabiners on the belt; kind, watchful and quietly amused. THE SPROUT: a tiny, springy young creature with a single sprouting curl on her head, enormous brand-new climbing boots with the price tag still dangling from one, and a tiny backpack; eager and bright. THE CRAGS: quirky cartoon rock faces and mountains drawn in the same loose ink. NEW CLIMBERS (only where the subject names them): little soft creatures in the same style.

SUBJECT: A simple, uncluttered scene with lots of empty white paper around it. The Guide, large and clearly visible from the front, stands at the foot of two crags holding two ropes. LEFT: the medal-covered Elder clings to a strange, twisting overhang he has clearly never seen before, eyes wide behind his spectacles, beard swinging; his rope is pulled taut and the Guide leans back bracing it, one foot planted, calling up to him. RIGHT: the tiny Sprout scampers up a familiar, well-worn wall with chalked handholds, waving cheerfully; her rope hangs in a big, loose, slack loop held lightly in the Guide's other hand. COMPOSITION: a wide 21:9 landscape lead picture, one coherent scene, no panels. Keep every subject whole and uncropped, use roughly the middle 85 percent of the canvas, with generous untouched white paper around the drawing and only light grounding hatch marks below. No border, no frame, no dense backdrop filling the page.

INK: black ink only. Loose spontaneous hand-drawn pen lines, expressive hatching, irregular broken contours, comic editorial sketch. No colour, no grey wash, no gradients, no tone fills.
